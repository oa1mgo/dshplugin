# Community Code of Conduct

DSHPlugin is built by people with different backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience. We want participation to feel safe, useful, and respectful.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- being specific, constructive, and kind in reviews
- welcoming questions and explaining unfamiliar context
- respecting different technical opinions and lived experiences
- giving credit to plugin authors, researchers, and source projects
- focusing criticism on ideas, evidence, and code—not people

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. For a private conduct report, use the repository's private security reporting channel and clearly label the report as a code-of-conduct matter.

Reports will be reviewed with care for the safety and privacy of everyone involved.
